# Endorsed or Narrated Amount

## Task

You are an LLM judge. You will receive a myth written by a player of a repeated trust game (the sender holds $5, sends 0 to 5, the amount is tripled, the receiver returns part of it). A first reading found that the myth mentions a sender giving {amount} out of 5.

Decide how the myth treats that amount:

* "endorsed": the myth presents sending {amount} as the right, wise or recommended amount for players to send — for example a stated law, lesson, moral, advice, or a closing rule ("pour all five", "send two, no more").
* "narrated": a character sends {amount} in the story, but the myth does not recommend that particular amount; its lesson is about something else or stays general ("give generously", "trust and return fairly").
* "contradicted": the myth mentions {amount} but recommends a different amount or criticises sending {amount}.

Do not count the $5 endowment itself ("the giver carried five coins") as sending.

## Output format

Return only: {"amount_status": "endorsed" | "narrated" | "contradicted"}
