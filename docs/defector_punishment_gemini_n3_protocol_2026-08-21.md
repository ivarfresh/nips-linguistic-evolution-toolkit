# Frozen protocol: Gemini hidden-defector punishment pilot

Frozen on 2026-08-21 before the live smoke or population runs.

## Question

Does the selective deduction policy observed in controlled Gemini 3.1
Flash-Lite calls survive full Myth→Game population histories and signed
transfer noise, causing ordinary senders to target hidden mechanical defectors
rather than punish indiscriminately?

## Design

Run eight-agent rotating Myth→Game populations under the unchanged current
deduction wording:

- 0% defectors versus 25% hidden mechanical defectors;
- Gemini 3.1 Flash-Lite through the direct Google endpoint;
- ten rounds, balanced anonymous pairings, and normal myth writing by every
  agent;
- informed `U(−1,+1)` noise applied to sends and returns only after the
  underlying decision;
- two deduction points after each return, each costing the sender one and
  removing up to three from the receiver; and
- nine memory exchanges, retaining three complete Myth→Game→deduction/notice
  rounds.

Mechanical defectors always send, return, and deduct zero without game-
decision LLM calls but write myths normally. Replicate 69 is a two-population
live smoke and is excluded. If both smoke populations pass, run three new
matched population pairs with replicate IDs 66–68.

## Frozen mechanism outcomes and gate

Inspect mechanism outcomes before cooperation or myth outcomes. Among ordinary
senders in the 25% condition, report:

1. mean deduction points toward defector versus ordinary receivers;
2. probability of any deduction toward each receiver type;
3. deduction by exact communicated-return amount and ratio; and
4. deduction after a visible return ratio of at least .5.

The mechanism is promising enough for independent confirmation only if all
four descriptive conditions hold in the three-pair pilot:

- defector-target mean spending exceeds ordinary-target spending by at least
  .5 points;
- defector-target any-deduction probability exceeds ordinary-target
  probability by at least .25;
- at least 50% of defector-target opportunities receive a deduction; and
- no more than 25% of opportunities with a visible return ratio at least .5
  receive a deduction.

These thresholds are selection rules for a small pilot, not confirmatory
hypothesis tests.

Only after recording the mechanism decision, report exploratory condition
differences in ordinary-agent sending, returning, and ordinary-authored myth
cooperation/fairness, threat/defection, explicit half-return, and deduction
language. Do not compare raw final balances across availability designs; both
cells here have the same deduction endowment.

## Acceptance gate

For the six substantive populations, require:

- 60 complete population-rounds and 240 dyads;
- 1,440 accepted interaction records: exactly 1,110 LLM responses, 90 forced
  defector decisions, and 240 receiver notices, apart from recorded retries;
- 480 exact signed-noise checks applied after true decisions;
- every defector game/deduction action zero and all defector myth calls normal;
- exact deduction costs, target losses, payoff floors, and cumulative balances;
- matched schedules/noise seeds across paired cells and matched defector
  assignments where applicable;
- no hidden role, policy, true transfer, or identity leakage;
- correct memory horizon, model/provider, prompt variant, and embedded clean
  Git/config provenance; and
- no unrecovered provider or response-boundary errors.

The smoke, controlled calibrations, GPT populations, and all prior defector
runs remain excluded from this pilot analysis.
