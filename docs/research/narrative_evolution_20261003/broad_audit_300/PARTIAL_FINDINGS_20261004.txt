Source-checked examples (purposive, not representative)

Original final-run SHA256 hashes were rechecked for A001 and A004, and all ten
source myths read. Source paths/agent IDs and experimental metadata are in
selection.json; full source text is retained locally in trajectories.json.

A004: main frontier Claude Opus 5, myth-first, ordinary author.
R1-2 portray retaliation after noisy returns as causing mutual withdrawal.
R3 ends: "Send all. Return past fairness. Blame the wind, never the hand."
R4 ends: "Punish patterns, never weather."
R5 narrates a man sending a thimble three seasons running, followed by others
letting his bucket hang empty. R6-7 instead describe four seasons then refusal.
R8 says: "and even then, load once more in full" despite repeated thimbles.
R9 prescribes "one last full basket, in case fear, not greed, was the thief."
R10 prescribes "send less, never nothing, for even a hoarder can learn to row."
Interpretation: punishment/withdrawal exceptions become explicit and change;
the path is not monotonically harsher or more complex. Numeric patience windows,
extra chances and limited sanctions appear. This is textual prescription, not
proof that the agent followed it or acquired it from a peer.

A001: main-frontier defector cohort, GPT-5.6 Sol, myth-first, ordinary author.
R1 already says to watch patterns and correct selfishness gently; this is not
a wholly unconditional baseline. Later myths elaborate caution, reopening and
role-sensitive judgment. R6: "A closed gate may guard the village," followed
by "but it must still possess a hinge." The following paragraph offers recovery
if the keeper later risks an honest ember. R10: "suffering betrayal is not
evidence of committing it." That is an explicit distinction between cautious
behavior following victimization and misconduct, not merely a fixed transfer.
Do not automatically label these elaborations first-ever conditionalization.

Synthesis: these examples distinguish accident from misconduct and consider
persistence, intention, recovery and the partner's circumstances. They support
an exploratory account of norm reasoning within myths. They do not establish
norm transmission, behavioral enactment, a frontier-versus-September difference,
or the mechanism causing the myth condition's cooperation effect.

Automatic feature labels remain fallible: for example A001R07's fairness
calculation was labeled hypothetical, which is not convincing under the strict
scenario definition. Source checks remain necessary across every theme.
