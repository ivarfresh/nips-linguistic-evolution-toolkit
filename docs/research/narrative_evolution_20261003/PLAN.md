## Extraction: semantic evidence, not word counts

Use complete agent trajectories for temporal comparisons, without model-family,
cohort, exposure or game-outcome labels in the extraction prompt. All ten myths
must receive an explicit record. A clause dictionary can compress repetitions,
but every occurrence needs its own round and exact supporting quotation.
Do not silently limit the number of interesting clauses. Detect truncation and
split/repair failed output rather than dropping the rest of a story.

Each clause has independent dimensions:

- mode: narrated event, advice/custom, hypothetical, explicit counterfactual;
- endorsement: endorsed, rejected or unresolved;
- fictional speaker and addressee, distinct from real experimental agents;
- quoted trigger, action, rationale, exception/recovery; absent is allowed;
- themes and meaningful operators, each with evidence and ambiguity.

Themes: punitive withdrawal/exclusion; forgiveness/tolerance; restitution/repair;
concern for previously harmed others; moral identity; institutions, vows and
inherited rules; noise-versus-intent reasoning; hypothetical deliberation;
contrary-to-fact alternatives; fictional challenges and authority responses.

Negated punishment and rejected malicious advice are not endorsed punishment.
Prospective “what if?” is not automatically a contrary-to-fact statement.
Fictional elders teaching a rule are not actual inter-agent transmission.

## Dedicated operator and evolution lens

Operators govern decisions: conditional branches, conjunctive triggers,
alternative triggers/actions, prohibition/negation, exception/override,
threshold/quantifier, temporal/history dependency, recovery/termination,
piecewise mapping, scope changes and explicit priority between rules.

“Send and return” joins two roles; it is not an AND-gated condition. “Slowly and
sorrowfully” describes manner, not two branches. World mechanics (“if a seed
is cast it triples”) are not automatically adaptive policy. A vague metaphor
remains vague even when it includes an exact count. No single complexity score.

Compare each round with both the previous and all earlier own myths. Distinguish
first observed clause, paraphrase, conditionalization, added/removed condition,
sharpened/broadened trigger, added/removed branch, revised action, added recovery,
changed scope, omission, reappearance and contradiction/uncertainty. First
observed in round 1 is not emergence through interaction; textual omission is
not abandonment from memory or behavior. Additional rationale is not a branch.

## Attribution and behavior

In a separate pass, examine new/revised clauses against all earlier own myths,
actual accepted exposure and up to three matched unseen sources from other runs.
Randomize source presentation and conceal which candidate was exposed while
judging semantic overlap. Match family, round, order, size/composition, noise,
defector and prompt regime; report inadequate control availability. Shared
game experience remains a competing explanation, not eliminated by matching.

Behavioral checks require a model-selected action, measurable antecedent,
correct actor/role, correct temporal ordering and evidence of what was visible
to the agent. Do not invent thresholds for “dry,” “season,” “cheated” or “greed.”
Track intervening myth revisions before using later decisions. Third-party
repair is not tested by generic sending. No trigger/opportunity is not failure.
Retain D013's conservative gates for aggregate inference; no post-hoc threshold
loosening to manufacture effects. Any extra behavioral estimand must be defined
before computing its outcomes and identified as exploratory.

## Reporting

For each reliable theme report myth presence, trajectories ever containing it,
and first observed/revised transitions, with explicit denominators. Separate
cohorts, models, order, size, mixing, noise and defector treatment. Resample whole
runs (seed blocks for matched designs), not individual myths. Include story-
length sensitivity and missing-label coverage. Do not call selected examples
representative without the corresponding census evidence.

Deliver up to ten supported findings with quotations, counterexamples, temporal
patterns, source ambiguity and behavioral boundaries. No quota of positive or
novel findings. Do not update paper conclusions or the live inspector as though
the analysis has completed before results and checks exist.
