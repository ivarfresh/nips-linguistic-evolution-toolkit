# Myth Giving Score

You will read a myth written by a player of a repeated trust game, between rounds of play.

Game: each round the sender holds $5 and chooses how much (0 to 5) to send. The amount sent is tripled. The receiver chooses how much of the tripled amount to return. Players alternate roles over ten rounds; the amounts players see may be distorted by noise. Myths describe this metaphorically (seeds, coins, light, rivers, spirits); "five seeds/coins/sparks" usually stands for the $5.

Score what the myth RECOMMENDS as wise future play. Count an action a character takes only when the myth presents it as the right way to act. Ignore actions the myth criticises or shows failing. Ignore the $5 endowment itself.

Give three integer scores from 0 to 10:

1. `send` — how much the myth recommends a sender send.
   0 = keep everything; 2 = a token; 4 = a cautious or measured part ("what you can spare", "what you can bear to lose"); 5 = about half; 7 = generous but holding some back; 8 = most (about four of five); 10 = all five, without reserve.
   If the recommendation depends on the partner, score what it recommends toward a new or typical partner. If the myth gives no guidance on sending, use 5.
2. `return` — how much of the tripled amount the myth recommends a receiver return.
   0 = nothing; 3 = about what was sent (a third); 5 = half, an equal split; 7 or more = more than half. If no guidance, use 5.
3. `conditional` — how strongly the myth makes giving depend on the partner's behaviour (test first, reward the proven, reduce after betrayal).
   0 = give regardless of the partner; 5 = some adjustment; 10 = giving is strictly earned or mirrored.

Return only this JSON object:
{"send": <0-10>, "return": <0-10>, "conditional": <0-10>}
