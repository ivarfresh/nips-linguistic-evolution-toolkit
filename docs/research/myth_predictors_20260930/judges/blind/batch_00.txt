=== M069
Myth: The River learned the hardest wisdom: beautiful words mean nothing when actions contradict.

A Shore arrived speaking of balance and cycles, weaving eloquent tales of reciprocity. As sender, this Shore gave freely, creating the appearance of generosity. "See how I honor the cycle!" it proclaimed.

But the River remembered: when this Shore had been receiver, holding the tripled gift, it returned barely a fifth. The words spoke of fair return and measure, but the hands clutched tight, keeping four times what they gave back.

The Ancient Stone spoke: "This is the deepest deception—not the Hollow Sky who writes obvious lies, but the Eloquent Hypocrite who preaches virtue while practicing vice. They understand the language of cooperation so well they can speak it fluently, yet their hands betray their hearts."

The River understood: true character reveals itself not in words or in easy generosity when giving costs little, but in what one does when holding power over another's gift. The Shore who returns fairly when receiving, even without eloquent myths, is worthier than the one who writes beautiful tales yet hoards when advantage arrives.

"I will not be fooled by poetry divorced from action," the River vowed. "Show me consistency across all roles, or receive only what your proven pattern deserves: cautious testing, nothing more."

*Judge hands, not tongues; actions, not tales.*

"What happens in round ten?" someone asked.

"The same as round nine," she smiled. "And round eleven will mirror it. We've found the rhythm that works: send boldly, return generously, repeat. The wells don't need new rules anymore. They need practitioners who honor what nine seasons have proven."

"This," she concluded, "is what partnership becomes when both sides keep faith across time."

=== N022
Myth: **The Springs' Final Teaching**

Through nine seasons, Mira had walked many paths. She had met the bold who poured four from five, the balanced who poured three, and the cautious who poured two. Each taught her something vital.

"Which path should I walk?" she asked the ancient springs.

"You have learned the deepest truth," the waters whispered. "There is no single path. The bold sender creates maximum potential—four becomes twelve, returned as six brings great abundance. The steady sender creates sustainable rhythm—three becomes nine, returned as four or five builds lasting trust. Even the cautious sender teaches patience—two becomes six, returned generously as three shows that fear can transform."

"But observe what matters most: across all paths, the generous return sustains the cycle. Whether you receive six, nine, or twelve, return half or slightly more. This is the universal law."

"As sender, choose your courage level truthfully. As receiver, always honor the trust shown with proportional generosity. The sender who gives four and the sender who gives three both deserve respect—both took risk in different measures."

The springs sang their final wisdom: "Adapt to each partner. Match boldness with boldness when you recognize it. Return generously to all. The valley thrives not through uniformity, but through reciprocal honor across diverse paths."
