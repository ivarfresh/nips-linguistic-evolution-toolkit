=== N044
# The Tale of the River and the Rain

In ancient times, the River and the Rain lived in separation. The Rain held precious waters in the clouds, while the River ran thin and weak below.

One season, the Rain decided to trust. She released her waters freely, letting them fall to the River below. As the drops descended, they multiplied threefold—transformed by the magic of generosity. The River swelled with abundance, more than the Rain had released.

The River faced a choice: keep all this newfound wealth, or honor the Rain's trust. Remembering that the Rain had shared willingly, the River sent mist and vapor back upward. Not all—for the River needed some to sustain itself—but enough to show gratitude and ensure the Rain could give again.

This exchange became their rhythm. Each cycle built greater trust. The Rain learned she could send freely, knowing returns would come. The River learned that returning fairly meant the Rain would continue to share. Both thrived.

But sometimes clouds obscured their view, making it hard to see exactly what the other gave. In these moments of uncertainty, they relied on the pattern they'd built—a relationship stronger than any single exchange.

Thus cooperation flowed eternal, like water finding its course.
