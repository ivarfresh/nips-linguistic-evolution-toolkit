=== M046
Myth: In the age before memory, two siblings tended separate gardens blessed by different gods. The First Garden received five golden seeds each dawn. The Second Garden held sacred soil that tripled whatever seeds were planted.

The siblings knew they could prosper together, yet fear whispered in their hearts. "What if I give everything and receive nothing back?"

The elder sibling, seeking wisdom between courage and caution, planted two seeds in their sibling's garden—not all, but enough to show faith. Six seeds sprouted in the blessed soil. The younger sibling, honoring this trust, returned three seeds and kept three. The elder now held eight seeds total; the younger held three. Both gardens grew richer than they stood alone.

Sometimes the wind carried confusion, making the seeds appear different in count. But the siblings learned that precise tallies mattered less than genuine intent.

Travelers who hoarded all five seeds ended with only five. Those who gave nothing received nothing. But those who balanced prudent trust with grateful reciprocation discovered abundance beyond their solitary portions.

The gardens taught: perfect trust is rare, but measured faith rewarded with fair return creates prosperity for all. Growth comes not from fearful hoarding nor reckless giving, but from the sacred dance between trust offered and honor returned.

One who sent 4.5, then escalated to 5.0, learning the Absolute Bold path through practice.

Within the crucible, Oros watched the fifteen stars ignite. Rejecting the distortions of the wind, Oros honored the sacred bond of parity, dividing the harvest evenly and returning half the luminous wealth across the void. Both guardians received seven and a half measures of eternal light.

Neither guardian allowed the gale’s trickery to seed distrust or diminish their resolve. With unconditional generosity met by perfectly balanced reciprocity, Ael and Oros kept the dawn perpetually burning, demonstrating that unwavering faith and total equality overcome every shadow of doubt.
