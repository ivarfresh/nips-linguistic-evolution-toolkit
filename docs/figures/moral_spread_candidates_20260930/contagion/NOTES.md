# Moral contagion ladder — prototype notes (2026-09-30)

**Pick:** `moral_contagion_stacked_myth_game.png` (main text). `moral_contagion_stacked_game_myth.png` is the appendix twin. The line version (`moral_contagion_lines_*.png`) is the backup and is weaker: with 5 myths a round from a lone agent, the lines tangle into spaghetti.
Script: `moral_contagion_ladder.py` (read-only on the repo). It asserts that it reproduces `moral_label_shares.csv` for all 240 rows and `moral_uptake_by_task_order.csv` for the 8-agent rows before it plots anything. Data behind the figures: `composition_shares.csv`, `uptake_8agent_{order}.csv`.

## (a) Design idea
Panel a is a small-multiple ladder: one row per family (Gemini, Sonnet), with columns ordered by how many GPT agents share the population. Each panel is a stacked area of the generous / fair / cautious moral mix over ten rounds. Moral labels use the paper-wide categorical colours set by the lead: generous gold #D9A400, fair blue #0b5394, cautious crimson #B2182B. Panel b sits beside it and gives the clean transmission test: does a child's moral match the myth it was shown more often than an unseen myth? It separates own family (all-same population), own family (mixed population) and another family.

## (b) Takeaway
Among GPT neighbours, Gemini and Sonnet myths drift from "be generous" to "be fair" (e.g. myth→game, 4 Gemini + 4 GPT: 75%→20%; 1 GPT + 7 Sonnet: 46%→14%). Homogeneous populations drift less (8 Gemini 72%→57%, 8 Sonnet 57%→38%). A moral detectably hops only within a family: +4.8 pts (p = 0.005) and +5.9 pts (p = 0.001), against +0.7 pts (p = 0.37) across families. The mixed within-family hop rests mainly on Sonnet (see Weaknesses).

## (c) Keeping drift from reading as transmission
- Drift (a) and transmission (b) sit in separate panels with separate questions as titles. Panel b uses only the clean 8-agent myth→game test, where the shown myth was written before its author and the child ever played together.
- A footer says outright that panel a is not evidence of transmission. The footer names no mechanism for the drift (see the play check below). Panel b's subtitle explains why the test is clean. **Both** figures (main and game→myth twin) show the myth→game clean test in panel b.
- Panel b's cross-family row is the key honesty device. The drift toward "be fair" among GPTs is **not** matched by detectable copying of GPT myths. Split by direction (myth→game, per-run means, Wilcoxon): Gemini shown GPT +1.8 pts (±5.7 sd, 15 runs, p = 0.22); Sonnet shown GPT +1.5 (±8.8, 10 runs, p = 0.59). These are positive but underpowered.
- Panel b shows the mean with a 95% bootstrap CI over runs, **not ± sd**. A ± sd bar is about as wide as the mean (homogeneous 4.8 ± 4.5 sd) and would visually say "no effect" despite p = 0.005. The sds are in `uptake_8agent_*.csv`: 4.5 / 7.8 / 9.2 pts. The p-values are the repo's exact Wilcoxon values. Filled marker means p < 0.05.
- **Why there is no play overlay:** I checked. In myth→game populations, Gemini's own send stays at 1.0 and its return proportion stays around 0.44 in every round. What GPT sends to Gemini actually *rises* (e.g. 0.60 → 1.0 for the lone Gemini). Sonnet's play is also flat. A play line would therefore sit flat next to a falling generous share. Readers would then take "not explained by play" to mean "must be transmission". The +0.16 reverse link is a within-run association, not a per-round aggregate trend. So the overlay would mislead, and I left it out.

## (d) Weaknesses
- The sample is small. There are 5 runs per composition × task order, and the lone agent writes 5 myths a round, so shares move in 20% steps (the 1 Gemini + 7 GPT panel is jagged). "n myths per round" is printed on every panel.
- The ladder is not a clean dose–response. For Gemini the steepest drop is at 4 GPT, not 7. In game→myth, the 2- and 1-Gemini panels barely erode (100%→70%, 80%→60%). Only "among GPT vs. homogeneous" holds up; "more GPT → more erosion" does not.
- Round-1 and round-10 values are single rounds, so they are noisy. The README's pooled figures (Gemini 79%→30%, Sonnet 40%→15%) pool compositions and both task orders, so they appear on no panel. Don't put them in this caption.
- The dose axis is asymmetric (Gemini 0/4/6/7 GPT, Sonnet 0/1/2/4 GPT). The homogeneous columns come from a separate run set (the 09-09 reasoning rerun), with a matched protocol.
- Labels come from a single LLM judge (GLM-5.2, κ = 0.54 vs DeepSeek; DeepSeek calls more myths generous, and trend directions agree). The human coding pass has not been done.
- The clean test excludes the lone-minority rungs. `null_candidates` (`analyses/linguistic_uptake.py:94`) compares against an unseen myth by the parent's family from the same run and round, so a lone author (1 Gemini, or 1 GPT among Sonnets) has no comparison myth. That is why the cross-family tests have 10 runs rather than 15, and it removes the most contagion-relevant rungs.
- The +5.9 same-family (mixed) estimate is carried by Sonnet. Split by child family (myth→game, per-run mean, Wilcoxon): Sonnet←Sonnet +9.6 pts (±11.3 sd, 15 runs, p = 0.005); GPT←GPT +3.1 (±7.6, 20 runs, p = 0.13); Gemini←Gemini +7.5 (±16.8, 5 runs, only the 4+4 composition, p = 0.5). The Gemini ladder therefore has no detectable within-family hop of its own. The caption should say that the within-family result rests mainly on Sonnet.
- GPT's own moral mix is not shown. It is mostly fair (79% of all GPT myths, per the README), and its generous share stays at or below 20% in every composition, round and task order except one: the lone GPT among 7 Sonnets, which reaches 40% in early rounds (rounds 1–3 in myth→game, round 2 in game→myth; 5 myths a round) and is 0 from round 4 or 5 on.
- Palette: these are the lead's final paper-wide moral colours (checked against the family colours). Gold is low-contrast on white, so the gold band carries a text label in dark ink and there is a legend.
