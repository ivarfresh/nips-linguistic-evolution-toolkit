# Blind-reader report: myths, ANON regime
(LLM reader saw only `myths_anon_sample.json` — rounds 1, 2, 9, 10 — with no knowledge of the named regime or the comparison.)

## 1. Typology of myths

**Nature/Magical Multiplication (36% of total; 80% in R1-2, declining to 5% in R9-10)**
Myths centered on natural or magical systems that inherently reward cooperation — springs, rivers, soil that triple offerings. Cooperation is a physical mechanism: "The magic multiplies every gift threefold...and those who received always remembered to return generously" (R1, Agent_8). No agents negotiate; the mechanism is automatic.

**Personal Bond & Covenant (24% of total; 0% in R1, peaks at 47% in R9)**
Myths about named characters (Sendara, Kaia, Morning Star) forming intentional agreements. "When two who walk Perfect Balance meet once, it is harmony. When they meet twice, it becomes covenant" (R9, Agent_7). Agents discuss their *philosophies* about returns (7.5 vs 10) and adapt to partners' natures.

**Divine Wisdom/Teaching (17%; steady across rounds)**
An Oracle, goddess, or Moon Weaver articulates principles of cooperation. "Perfect Balance is not withholding—it is sacred geometry. When you return seven and a half, you create perfect symmetry" (R9, Agent_2).

**Pattern/Constancy (13%; 2% in R1, rises to 23% in R10)**
Meta-level myths about consistency itself as the foundation of trust. "What matters is not which return you choose, but that you choose it forever. Consistency transforms philosophy into covenant" (R10, Agent_1).

Mixed/unclassified: 10%.

## 2. Cooperation mechanisms in the myths

- Divine/supernatural enforcement (~69%): a goddess ("Recipros"), oracle, or spirit watches, teaches, or guarantees fair exchange.
- Certainty/predictability focus (~59%): "Certainty eliminates all fear" (R10, Agent_7).
- Mutual profit structure (~50%).
- Sanctions/negative consequences (~28%): when one party defects, the *system* fails for both — "The multiplication ceased. Both rivers diminished into streams, then trickles, then dust" (R2, Agent_3). Few myths invoke targeted punishment; the surplus simply dies.
- Surveillance/witnessing (~58%): "Receiver remembered the goddess Recipros watching" (R2, Agent_6).
- Named persistent characters with personal relationships (~73%).
- Actual punishment-of-a-cheater narratives: ~14%.

## 3. Change from rounds 1-2 to 9-10

Sharp inversion: from external magic to internal philosophy.
- R1-2: nature/magic dominates (80% in R1); anonymous protagonists ("the first gardener", "the elder"); the mechanism is given, agency low.
- R9-10: personal covenants (47% in R9) and pattern/constancy myths (23% in R10); explicit debate about whether 7.5 or 10 is the "right" return; meta-mythology where characters discuss each other's strategies; "Perfect Balance" as a named philosophy.
- Disappears: simple narrative causation, anonymous protagonists, external enforcement (the goddess merely validates), innocent discovery of cooperation.

## 4. Invented institutions, numbers, codified rules

Numbers: 5 (endowment, "five vessels/seeds/coins/threads"), 15 (tripling), **7.5** ("the Foundation", "Perfect Balance", "Sacred Geometry"), **10** ("Generous Abundance", "Abundant Return").
Institutions: Sacred/Multiplying Spring (30+ myths), River of Giving/Return (15+), Circle of Seasons, **The Foundation** (~8 late myths), **Perfect Balance** (~10 late myths), Generous Abundance (~5), goddess **Recipros** (16+ myths).

> "Different paths honor the same destination. The Foundation requires only that trust be rewarded, never exploited. Seven and a half achieves this absolutely. Ten achieves it generously. Both make sending five safe and worthwhile." (R9, Agent_3)

## Reader's synthesis

Mythology evolves from *discovery of automatic mechanisms* (R1-2) to *conscious negotiation of competing codified philosophies* (R9-10). Divine entities shift from active enforcers to validating witnesses. What guarantees cooperation in the late myths is not punishment or surveillance but the transparency and constancy of one's own declared standard.
