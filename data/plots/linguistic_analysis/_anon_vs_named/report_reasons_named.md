## 1. Typology of decision strategies

- **Pattern matching (~52%)**: explicit reference to the counterpart's historical sends/returns to calibrate the current move ("In Round 1 they returned $8, so I'll do the same").
- **Norm enforcement (~48%)**: invocation of explicit shared conventions ("Golden Standard", "covenant", "sacred mathematics") justifying specific quantities ($10 from $15, $5 sends, 50% returns) — operating as internalized norms rather than threats.
- **Mythic justification (<1% as primary)**, though myths regularly support the above.
- Generic fairness/honor appeals appear in ~90% of traces.

## 2. Enforcement, norms, track record

- **Conditional language in ~17.5%** of traces, but almost entirely mythic/descriptive ("When one guardian breaks faith, the crystal stream turns to stone") rather than threats. **Actual enforcement intent ~2-3%.**
- **Appeals to shared norms with quantities: ~52%** — "Golden Standard" (send $5, return $10) in ~10%; "covenant" in ~23%; returning "half" ($7.5) in ~55%; complete-trust framing in ~62%. Treated as discovered shared norms ("Galen follows the Golden Standard, so must I").
- **Partner's individual track record: ~100% of traces.** Every reasoning opens with a named, quantified history: "Looking at Elara's history: Round 1 sent $4 (80%), returned $7 from $12 (58%)..."

## 3. How counterparts are referred to

- **By individual name: 100%** (Elara, Dorian, Galen, …).
- By mythic archetype (~24%): Keeper, Spring, Guardian, Path walker.
- By characteristic (~26%): "proven", "high-cooperator", "trustworthy".
- Partners are individuals with known trajectories, not fungible strangers.

## Reader's summary

Decisions run through named partners' quantified track records (pattern matching ~52%) and discovered numerical norms (~48%). Enforcement is largely absent; agents describe what *would break* the covenant mythically but rarely threaten retaliation. Agents are tracking individuals, not playing against an anonymous population.
