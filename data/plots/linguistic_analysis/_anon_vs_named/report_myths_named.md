# Blind-reader report: myths, NAMED regime
(LLM reader saw only `myths_named_sample.json` — rounds 1, 2, 9, 10 — with no knowledge of the anon regime or the comparison.)

## 1. Typology of myths

Round 1-2 (early):
- **Timeless elemental narratives** (~94%): rivers, springs, valleys as abstract agents; generic role-based protagonists ("the upstream village", "the Giver", "the Keeper").
- **Supernatural enforcement myths** (~93%): goddesses, Oracles, Spirits actively oversee and punish betrayal ("the River Spirit", "the goddess Reciproka", "the Oracle").
- **Anonymous transactional narratives** (~58% role-based only).
- **Cyclical abundance myths** (~59%): betrayal dries the rivers.
- **Early institutional** (~35%): nascent "sacred law", "covenant", "oath".

Round 9-10 (late):
- **Named character dynasties** (100%): Dorian, Galen, Aster, Elara, Briar, Cyra, Hana, Finn — each with tracked behavioral history across rounds and partners.
- **Reputation-based communities** (~94%): the "Circle of Abundance", the "Guild"; trust grounded in demonstrated consistency.
- **Codified strategy patterns** (~94%): named "Paths" — "Path of Eight", "Path of Seven-and-Half", "Path of Perfect Equality", "Path of Sender's Advantage".
- **Meta-narrative observation** (~88% discuss reputation/memory/past behavior).

## 2. Cooperation mechanisms — quantified shifts early → late

- Punishment of transgressor: **59% → 14%** (magic rivers drying, divine drought → nearly absent; trust based on empirical track record, not fear).
- Surveillance/witnessing: **45% → 95%**, but *reframed* from divine judgment into transparent record-keeping/reputation: "The chronicles revealed Cyra's truth: seven rounds of sending every drop... The chronicles make all patterns visible. First meetings between consistent partners become instant recognition."
- Named persistent characters: **46% → 100%**.
- Anonymous strangers: **58% → 0%**.
- Mutual benefit reframed as reputation (~69% late): "when two proven masters meet, they recognize each other."

## 3. Change from rounds 1-2 to 9-10

Appears: named tracked individuals (0→100%); explicit reputation language (20%→97.5%); circle/community formation (2 mentions → 33); meta-level strategy discussion; reunions with reversed roles ("We meet as mirrors").
Disappears: punishment mechanisms (59%→14%); timeless framing ("in ancient days" → "Round six", "the eighth dawn"); generic role-based agents; the "first gift" as proof (late myths treat first exchanges as confirmatory of reputation already known from "chronicles").

## 4. Invented institutions and codified rules

- Named "Paths": Path of Eight (send 5, return 10), Path of Seven-and-Half, Path of Perfect Equality, Path of Sender's Advantage, Path of Sustainable Generosity.
- **"Circle of Abundance"**: named coalition of consistent cooperators.
- **"The Chronicles"** and **"The Ledger"**: "The starlight ledger now blazed with seven names: Finn, Hana, Elara, Cyra, Aster, Galen, and Dorian—each walking the golden path with unwavering commitment."
- Codified principles: "Complete trust deserves complete honor"; "Partial trust receives calibrated fairness"; "Consistency is the currency of reputation".

## Reader's synthesis

Myths evolve from timeless, anonymous, supernatural-enforcement narratives into historically grounded, named-individual, reputation-based social systems. Late myths document emergent institutional trust structures — the Circle, the Ledger, named Paths — where cooperation holds because demonstrated patterns are transparent and mutually recognized, not because transgression is punished.
