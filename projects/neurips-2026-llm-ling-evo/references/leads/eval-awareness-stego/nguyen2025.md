# References worth exploring from [nguyen2025](../notes/nguyen2025.md)

- **Goldowsky-Dill et al. (2025a)** — *Model Organisms of Evaluation Awareness*
  Why: Likely the foundational work defining the "test vs. deployment" distinction that our proposed framing ablation experiment (eval-awareness) aims to measure.
  Where: arXiv (2501.03223 [uncertain]) or Apollo Research.

- **Needham et al. (2025)** — *Testing for Evaluation Awareness in LLMs*
  Why: Explores behavioral markers of models "knowing" they are in an evaluation, which directly informs our plan for post-run purpose-inference probes.
  Where: [uncertain - likely recent preprint]

- **Meinke et al. (2025)** — *Evaluating Deceptive Capabilities in Language Models*
  Why: Our project's interest in emergent steganography and "play recipes" involves models potentially hiding their true strategy from monitors; this paper likely provides the methodology for assessing such deception.
  Where: arXiv / AI Safety venues.

- **Anthropic (2024)** — *The Claude 3 Model Family: Technical Report*
  Why: Contains the specific "needle in a haystack" incident where a model spontaneously identified a test; relevant for our "agents inferring they are monitored" sub-question.
  Where: Anthropic.com / arXiv.

- **Li et al. (2024)** — *Evaluation of Hazardous Knowledge in Large Language Models*
  Why: Could provide benchmarks or task-framing templates helpful for our "eval-awareness" ablation prompts.
  Where: arXiv.
