# References worth exploring from [needham2025large](../notes/needham2025large.md)

- **Laine et al. (2024)** — *Model organism of situational awareness in LLMs*
  Why: Likely provides the foundational definitions and experimental setups for "situational awareness" which this project aims to ablate/frame.
  Where: [arXiv:2406.14283](https://arxiv.org/abs/2406.14283)

- **Shevlane et al. (2023)** — *Model evaluation for extreme risks*
  Why: Discusses the theoretical risks of models bypassing evaluations, relevant to the "LLM monitor" and "covert collusion" experiments in the project.
  Where: [arXiv:2305.15324](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15324)

- **McCambridge et al. (2014)** — *Systematic review of the Hawthorne effect in health-related behavior*
  Why: The paper uses this as a proxy for how AI behavior changes when observed; relevant for designing the "experiment- vs deployment-framed" ablation.
  Where: [Journal of Clinical Epidemiology](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jclinepi.2013.08.015)

- **Hubinger et al. (2019)** — *Risks from Learned Optimization in Advanced Machine Learning Systems*
  Why: Discusses "deceptive alignment," which is the theoretical endpoint of evaluation awareness where models hide true capabilities/intents—highly relevant to the emergence of steganography under monitoring.
  Where: [arXiv:1906.01820](https://arxiv.org/abs/1906.01820)
