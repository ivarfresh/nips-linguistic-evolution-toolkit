# References worth exploring from [roger2023](../notes/roger2023.md)

- **Lanham et al. (2023)** — *Measuring Faithfulness in Chain-of-Thought Reasoning*
  Why: Provides the foundational definition of "encoded reasoning" and the methodology for testing if models are sensitive to specific phrasing for downstream task performance.
  Where: arXiv:2307.13702

- **Perez et al. (2022)** — *Discovering Language Model Behaviors with Model-Written Evaluations*
  Why: Discusses "sycophancy" and the tendency of models to follow user preferences over truth; highly relevant to the "eval-awareness" framing and how models adapt behavior to perceived monitoring.
  Where: arXiv:2212.09251

- **Radhakrishnan et al. (2023)** — *Question Decomposition Improves the Faithfulness of Model-Generated Reasoning*
  Why: Explores unfaithful reasoning where LLMs ignore their own intermediate steps—useful for the "epiphenomenal vs load-bearing" question in the project's linguistic analysis.
  Where: arXiv:2307.11761

- **Wei et al. (2023)** — *Simple synthetic data can induce sycophancy in large language models*
  Why: Relevant to the project's goal of checking how "deployment-framed" prompts (eval-awareness) change strategic cooperation.
  Where: arXiv:2305.18248
