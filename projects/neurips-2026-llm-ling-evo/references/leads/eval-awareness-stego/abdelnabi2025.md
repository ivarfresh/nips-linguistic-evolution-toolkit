# References worth exploring from [abdelnabi2025](../notes/abdelnabi2025.md)

- **Adair (1984)** — *The Hawthorne effect: A reconsideration of the methodological artifact*
  Why: Provides the foundational psychological theory for the "test awareness" phenomenon, which could help frame why LLM agents might behave differently in a "game" context vs. a "natural" communication context in the trust game.
  Where: Journal of Applied Psychology

- **Hubinger et al. (2019)** — *Risks from Learned Optimization in Advanced Machine Learning Systems*
  Why: Likely discusses "situational awareness" and models "playing along" with training/eval processes—directly relevant to the project's interest in agents inferring they are being monitored.
  Where: arXiv:1906.01820

- **Perez et al. (2022)** — *Discovering Language Model Behaviors with Model-Written Evaluations*
  Why: Relevant to the project's goal of "purpose-inference probes" and how models might respond to being evaluated or "instrumentally" complying with perceived goals.
  Where: arXiv:2212.09251

- **Li et al. (2024)** — *The Geometry of Truth: Emergent Linear Structure in Learned Representations*
  Why: The white-box probing methodology used in Abdelnabi et al. likely draws on this work; useful for the project's planned experiments on probing for evaluation-awareness activations.
  Where: arXiv:2310.06824

- **Burns et al. (2022)** — *Discovering Latent Knowledge in Language Models Without Supervision*
  Why: Useful for the proposed "post-run purpose-inference probes" to see if agents "know" they are in an experiment even if they don't explicitly say so.
  Where: arXiv:2212.03827
