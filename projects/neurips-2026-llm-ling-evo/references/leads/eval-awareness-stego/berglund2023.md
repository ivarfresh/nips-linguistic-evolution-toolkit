# References worth exploring from [berglund2023](../notes/berglund2023.md)

- **Armstrong et al. (2020)** — *Pitfalls of Learning a Reward Function Online*
  Why: Relevant to the project's interest in how models behave when they know they are being monitored by a reward model/judge.
  Where: arXiv:2004.14547

- **Perez et al. (2022)** — *Discovering Language Model Behaviors with Model-Written Evaluations*
  Why: Provides methodology for "eval-awareness" framing and how models might behave differently when they recognize they are in a test setting.
  Where: arXiv:2212.09251

- **Kottardi et al. (2023) [uncertain]** — *An Overview of Catastrophic AI Risks*
  Why: Likely contains the theoretical grounding for "deceptive alignment," which matches the project's "covert collusion" and "steganography" research tracks.
  Where: arXiv:2306.12001

- **Hubinger et al. (2019)** — *Risks from Learned Optimization in Advanced Machine Learning Systems*
  Why: The foundational paper on "inner alignment" and "traps," providing the theoretical basis for why an agent might hide its true strategy (steganography) from a monitor.
  Where: arXiv:1906.01820
