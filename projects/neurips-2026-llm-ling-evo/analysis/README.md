## Headline findings

See `HEADLINE_TABLES.md` for tables and `RESULTS_SCAFFOLD.md` for prose.
Three-line summary:

1. Adding myth-writing **lifts and consolidates** cooperation in 3 of 21
   cells (all with upward-biased noise: positive ±$1) and **destabilises
   or harms** in 4 cells (all GPT-5-Nano × bootstrap noise). 10 cells
   are null.

2. Lag-1 cross-agent cooperativity-language correlations are **positive
   in every cell** (mean Pearson r per dyad: 0.27–0.58 on the stronger
   direction, with 27–73% of dyads showing |r|>0.5). The pilot Claude
   r ≈ 0.72 finding is representative, not anomalous.

3. **Claude's** game-response prose references the agent's own myth
   vocabulary in **78–82% of rounds**; **GPT-5-Nano** produces no
   visible reasoning prose at all (bare JSON action only), so
   the cross-task channel is undecidable for that model from the visible
   output. Coinages from myths do *not* leak into game `reason` text in
   any cell — pilot finding does not replicate at scale.
